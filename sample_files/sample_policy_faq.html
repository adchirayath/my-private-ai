1. Remote Work & Core Hours

Policy Summary: All full-time employees are eligible for hybrid or fully remote work subject to manager approval.

  • Core Collaboration Hours: 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST (all team members must be reachable on Slack during this window).
  • Remote Work Stipend: Every new remote employee receives a one-time equipment grant of $750 USD for home office setup.
  • Internet Subsidy: Acme reimburses home high-speed broadband up to $60 USD per month via the expense portal.

3. IT Security, Hardware & Password Compliance

All corporate devices, laptops, and data repositories must comply with Acme's ISO-27001 baseline security controls:

  1. Password Requirements: Minimum 16 characters including uppercase letters, numbers, and special symbols. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days.
  2. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Hardware security keys (YubiKey) or app-based authenticator (Duo Mobile) required. SMS verification is strictly prohibited.
  3. Device Encryption: Full-disk encryption (FileVault for macOS, BitLocker for Windows) must remain enabled at all times.
  4. Public Wi-Fi Restrictions: Employees working from cafes, airports, or hotels must connect via the corporate VPN (Gatekeeper-VPN) before accessing internal networks or databases.
